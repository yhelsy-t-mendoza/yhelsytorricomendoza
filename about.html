Biography

I graduated as valedictorian from Santa Rosa de Lima School (Fe y Alegría) in Bolivia with a 99.21/100 GPA, and received the Bolivian Ministry of Education's "Incentive to the Outstanding Baccalaureate." Since 2019, I've combined academic research with community organizing: I founded two youth organizations, took part in 30+ Model United Nations conferences, and I'm currently completing an independent research project alongside an internship at a departmental electoral court.

I'm passionate about the intersection of data and public policy — how electoral behavior, education policy, and gender representation can be studied with rigorous evidence and, at the same time, translated into concrete projects for young people in communities like Plan 3000 or the municipality of La Guardia. I'm currently preparing to apply again to universities in the United States.
